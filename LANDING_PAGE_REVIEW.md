## 3. Cognitive Science Diagnosis

### Eyebrow Badge
`COGNITIVE SCIENCE DIAGNOSIS`

### Title
## There is a fundamental problem with how we try to remember things.

### Narrative
Most people attempt to memorize long-form material by simply staring at it until their eyes ache, a strategy known as "massed passive review".

### Diagnostic 01: Passive Review
- **Title**: Passive Review
- **Body**: When we read a sixty-line lyric sheet top-to-bottom over and over, our eyes easily recognize the familiar typography on the page, creating what psychologists call the illusion of competence where visual recognition is mistaken for memory, leaving us stranded the moment we look away from the paper.
- **Result**: **Result: Over-rehearsed beginnings, forgotten middles, stage anxiety.**

### Diagnostic 02: Flashcards
- **Title**: Flashcards
- **Body**: While single-concept flashcards work well for vocabulary, they shatter the musical and temporal momentum of performance by training phrases as isolated islands rather than training the end of one verse to trigger the breath and melody of the next.
- **Result**: **Result: Knowing individual sections in theory, but choking on the transitions in practice.**

## 4. By Heart's Solution: Three Integrated Modalities

### Eyebrow Badge
`By Heart's Solution: Three Integrated Modalities` (with portrait line break)

### Title
## Engineered for each stage of human memory consolidation.

### Intro
By Heart replaces passive review with strategies that mirror how the human brain actually encodes, sequences, and retrieves motor-cadence memory under performance conditions.

### Modality 01: Chain & Loop Mode
- **Concept**: Guides you through small brain-sized chunks, (1 → 2 → (1+2) → 3 → (1+2+3)), building up until you get the whole thing down.
- **Why it beats staring & flashcards**: Instead of over-rehearsing the opening and stumbling on transitions, forward chaining welds adjacent sections together into a continuous motor chain where the end of one phrase automatically cues the start of the next.

### Modality 02: Word Scaffold Mode
- **Concept**: An interactive vanishing-cue system that masks non-essential words while keeping first-word anchors and syllable counts so your brain reconstructs the phrase from memory.
- **Why it beats staring & flashcards**: Instead of your eyes leaning passively on printed text, word scaffolding strips away textual crutches while leaving just enough phonetic priming to prevent freezing.

### Modality 03: Boundary Flashcards
- **Concept**: Smart turnaround cards generated from your song’s structure, testing whether the final line of a verse triggers the entrance to the chorus cold.
- **Why it beats staring & flashcards**: Instead of relying on starting from line one every time, boundary cards simulate live performance by training you to enter any section cold without hesitation.
